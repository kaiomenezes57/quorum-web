About Quorum

A modern platform for creating polls, collecting opinions, and making decisions with confidence.

Our Mission

Quorum makes online voting simple, fast, and accessible. Whether you're making a team decision, gathering community feedback, or creating a fun poll for friends, Quorum helps everyone participate effortlessly.

We believe creating a poll should take seconds, and voting should be intuitive on every device.
