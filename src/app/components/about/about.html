Our Mission

Quorum makes online voting simple, fast, gamified and accessible. Whether you're making a team decision, gathering community feedback, or creating a fun poll for friends, Quorum helps everyone participate effortlessly.

We believe creating a poll should take seconds, and voting should be intuitive on every device.
